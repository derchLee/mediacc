## Clusters and page mapping

- Image-converter pillar: `/image`; lead with HEIC to JPG, batch conversion, no upload.
- HEIC cluster: `/image/heic-to-jpg`, `/image/heic-to-png`, `/image/heic-to-webp`.
- Web-image cluster: JPG/PNG/WebP pairwise landing pages.
- Video-converter pillar: `/video`; lead with video to MP4, MOV to MP4, no upload.
- MP4 cluster: MOV/WebM/MKV/AVI to MP4 landing pages.
- Privacy proof: one reusable explainer showing how local browser processing can be verified.

## Measurement plan

- Connect Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

- Track `file_upload`, conversion start, completion, and download as a funnel; optimize for completed downloads, not visits alone.
- Re-score priorities once at least 28 days of query data is available.

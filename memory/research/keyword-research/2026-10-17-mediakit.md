# MediaKit keyword research and Search Console setup

Date: 2026-10-17. Target: mediakit.upupdays.com. Initial scope: English organic search; other locales require separate validation.

## Evidence and limits

Repository has image/video tools and 14 conversion definitions (9 image, 5 video). At the initial audit, URL defaults referenced the legacy host. The subsequent authorized migration updated defaults to https://mediakit.upupdays.com and verified 68 sitemap URLs and their generated page titles/canonicals in a successful local production build. Live deployment, DNS, redirects and account settings remain unverified. No Search Console export or Keyword Planner measurements available. All keyword volumes and SEO difficulty: N/A. Priorities below are qualitative product-fit judgments, not measured popularity or ranking forecasts.

1. Quick win: improve 3 existing pages: HEIC to JPG, WebP to JPG, WebP to PNG. Clear task titles, unique instructions/limits and tool access.

## Search Console handoff

- Domain property: upupdays.com (DNS verification), optionally mediakit.upupdays.com for product scope.
- URL prefix property: https://mediakit.upupdays.com/ for product monitoring.
- Sitemap: https://mediakit.upupdays.com/sitemap.xml; verify its entries use the new canonical hostname first.
- Inspect /image, /video, /image/heic-to-jpg, /image/webp-to-jpg, /image/webp-to-png; request indexing if eligible, no guarantee of inclusion.
- For an old-domain move: same-path permanent redirects, updated internal/canonical/hreflang/sitemap URLs, both sites verified; submit Change of Address from the applicable old property after redirects work.
- Search Console has no target-keyword submission. Google ignores meta keywords for indexing/ranking.
- Obtain monthly search estimates in Google Ads Keyword Planner, separated by country/language; advertiser Competition is not organic SEO difficulty.
